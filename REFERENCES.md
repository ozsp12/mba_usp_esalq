# References

This file consolidates the bibliography used in the MBA final project on clustering retail-bank branches and generating synthetic data. The entries have been standardized and grouped by subject.

## Clustering Methods

- MacQueen, J. B. (1967). Some methods for classification and analysis of multivariate observations. In L. M. Le Cam and J. Neyman (Eds.), *Proceedings of the Fifth Berkeley Symposium on Mathematical Statistics and Probability: Volume 1, Statistics* (pp. 281–297). University of California Press.

- Domeniconi, C., Gunopulos, D., Ma, S., Yan, B., Al-Razgan, M., and Papadopoulos, D. (2007). Locally adaptive metrics for clustering high-dimensional data. *Data Mining and Knowledge Discovery, 14*(1), 63–97. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10618-006-0060-8

- Tang, G., Tian, R., and Wu, B. (2022). An overview of clustering methods in the financial world. In *Proceedings of the 2022 7th International Conference on Financial Innovation and Economic Development (ICFIED 2022)*. Advances in Economics, Business and Management Research. https://doi.org/10.2991/aebmr.k.220307.084

## Banking and Financial Applications

- Herrera-Restrepo, O., Triantis, K., Seaver, W. L., Paradi, J. C., and Zhu, H. (2016). Bank branch operational performance: A robust multivariate and clustering approach. *Expert Systems with Applications, 50*, 107–119. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eswa.2015.12.025

- Marques, B. P., and Alves, C. F. (2020). Using clustering ensemble to identify banking business models. *Intelligent Systems in Accounting, Finance and Management, 27*(2), 66–94. https://doi.org/10.1002/isaf.1471

- Sharahi, M., and Aligholi, M. (2015). Classify the data of bank customers using data mining and clustering techniques: Case study of Sepah Bank branches in Tehran. *Journal of Applied Environmental and Biological Sciences, 5*(5), 458–464.

## Synthetic Data and Privacy

- Raghunathan, T. E. (2021). Synthetic data. *Annual Review of Statistics and Its Application, 8*, 129–140. https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-statistics-040720-031848

- Dankar, F. K., and Ibrahim, M. (2021). Fake it till you make it: Guidelines for effective synthetic data generation. *Applied Sciences, 11*(5), Article 2158. https://doi.org/10.3390/app11052158

- Hradec, J., Craglia, M., Di Leo, M., De Nigris, S., Ostlaender, N., and Nicholson, N. (2022). *Multipurpose synthetic population for policy applications* (EUR 31116 EN; JRC128595). Publications Office of the European Union. https://doi.org/10.2760/50072
